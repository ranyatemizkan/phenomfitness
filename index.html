Ranya Temizkan — IFBB Pro The Story Ranya Temizkan — The Phenom ￼ 2017 Fell in love with training — began competitive bodybuilding journey 2018 First NPC competition — Figure division 2021 Competed in NPC Wellness division 2025 WPD/WBB debut — Won Overall Women's Bodybuilding at NPC USA's, earned IFBB Pro Card 2025 Pro debut — Placed 4th at the 2025 Tampa Pro Next The Olympia stage — the ultimate goal Her Journey Built From Passion, Defined by Discipline Before the stage lights, before the trophies, before the pro card — there was a dancer. Ranya Temizkan spent years honing her body through the art of movement, developing the discipline, body awareness, and stage presence that would later set her apart in the world of competitive bodybuilding. In 2017, Ranya discovered a new passion: the iron. What began as a love affair with training quickly evolved into something far greater. She stepped onto the NPC stage for the first time in 2018 in the Figure division, planting the seed of a competitive career that would grow into something remarkable. After competing in NPC Wellness in 2021, Ranya took time to refine her physique, deepen her coaching knowledge, and prepare for her most ambitious chapter yet. In 2025, she returned to the stage in the Women's Physique and Women's Bodybuilding divisions — and she did not just compete. She dominated. At the 2025 NPC USA Championships, Ranya claimed the Overall Women's Bodybuilding title, earning her coveted IFBB Professional Card in the process. Just one week later, she stepped onto the professional stage for the very first time at the 2025 Tampa Pro, placing an impressive 4th in her debut against the world's elite. The road ahead leads to one destination: the Olympia stage. Follow along as Ranya continues to push the boundaries of what is possible — in the gym, on stage, and in the lives of the athletes she coaches every day.
